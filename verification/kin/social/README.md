# Social week (`node scripts/kin-model.ts social`)

**Rules.** Nothing reaches the posting service without the owner's Confirm on that exact post, and a post edited after its card was drawn needs a new card and a new Confirm. A post goes only to an account in its own group's profile. A removed post is never scheduled. A client's post needs the client's OK on its card. Restarts and lost answers never put a post at the service twice, and never leave it on Scheduling. Cancelled, Posted, and "wasn't sent" on the card are true at the service. An Undo Kin took cancels the post unless the service couldn't be reached before its time. Retry on a late post sends it now.
**Harm guarded.** A post goes out unasked, twice, edited behind the owner's back, or on another client's account; the card says cancelled or not sent while the post is live.
**Bound.** Three posts: the company's first (every action), a second on the same card (shown, removed or confirmed only), and a client's. At most two of these, once each: an answer lost, a restart, a platform failure, the company's account moved into the client's profile at the service, a key widened to every profile. About 297,000 states, 110 s. `--variants` breaks each rule on purpose (12/12 caught).
**Layers.** Wrong-group posting has three guards: social_draft refuses an account outside the group, Confirm checks the card's accounts against the group's, and the hand-over checks that the group's key reaches exactly its profile and that the account is in it now (the service's key scope is the last wall). The model keeps only the send-time check as a variant (`no-send-check`), because each earlier one alone is still caught at send time. An edit is guarded twice too: it puts the post back to draft and replaces the card, and Confirm compares the rows with the card's digest.
**Timing.** Undo closes 5 minutes before a post's time and a tap starts a pass at once, so the model lets the time come only after that pass tried to cancel. A cancel that fails with an unknown outcome stays Undoing and is tried again on the next pass, up to 5 minutes later; if the post went out meanwhile, the card goes back to Scheduled, says Undo didn't go through, and then shows Posted.
**Fixed.** A post whose time passed while Kin was off said "it wasn't sent" even when Kin had handed it over and lost the answer; it now says Kin can't tell (variant `old-late-message`). A post refused because its account left the group's profile said the same "nothing was sent" after such a lost answer; it now says an earlier attempt may have reached the service (variant `old-account-message`). Retry kept the first Confirm's time, so a late post failed as late again, and a late post's status was never read after Retry; Retry now confirms anew (variant `late-retry-stuck`). The service id is saved even when an Undo landed during the hand-over, and before an Undo's cancel, so a cancel that fails leaves a post Kin still reads. A saved id is read before anything is retried, so a restart between saving it and its state doesn't loop on the service's refusal. An Undo that must first find what an earlier attempt made asks with a future time, so a post that was never made can't publish before Kin cancels it (both outside the model's abstraction of the service: found by review, pinned in `Social.test.ts`).
**Left out.** Drafting itself, media checks, previews, packs and reminders (no posting). The service's own retries. Several cards per group: a newer card replaces the older one, and only proposed posts move.
