# Changing who a person is (`node scripts/kin-model.ts people`)

**Rules.** Once Ana is made a member again or removed, nothing owner-level reaches her: no read, no tap on a card she could confirm a minute ago, no turn that started while she was a co-owner. Made a co-owner, she gets a co-owner's view on her next request without joining again. With her Money place off and no project she leads, her next read and her running turn show no money. Removing her changes her access only: a task she held stays hers, for the owner to give to someone else, and no agent is woken by it. While removed she holds no level anywhere. Let back in, she comes back with the level and places she had, or what the owner changed them to while she was gone, and only the owner's link (or his making her a co-owner meanwhile) brings back a co-owner. A co-owner never changes anyone's kind, and there is always exactly one owner.
**Harm guarded.** A demoted or removed person keeps seeing or confirming owner-level things; removing someone silently reshuffles their work or starts agents on it; coming back loses who they were, or a co-owner restores another co-owner's level.
**Bound.** One person (Ana) beside the owner and a co-owner; one removal, two joins; one owner-level card in her chat, one turn, one task an agent gives her. About 6,000 states, 2 s. `--variants`: 10/10 caught.
**Fixed.**

1. `TasksStay` (owner's decision, replacing "a removed person's tasks go back to whoever gave them"): (1) An agent gives Ana a task. (2) You remove her. The task went back to the agent, which woke and worked on it. `removeAccess` now leaves her open tasks in her name and logs an `accessRemoved` change on each; the `holder_removed` pulse fact shows them under Needs you, to the owner only. Variant `removal-hands-back`.
2. `ComesBackAsShe` (owner's decision, replacing "she comes back as a new joiner"): (1) You make Ana a co-owner with Money on. (2) You remove her. (3) Your link brings her back: she was a member with a new joiner's places, out of her chats. `removeAccess` keeps a snapshot (`kin_people.removed_access_json`, K157); `accept` on a link naming her runs `bringBack`, which restores her level, places, lead access and chat memberships left at the removal. Variant `rejoin-as-new`.
3. `RemovedHoldsNoLevel`: the snapshot is the only place her level lives while she is gone; her row is a member's. Variant `removal-keeps-level`.
4. `OwnerBringsBackOwnerLevel`: (1) You make Ana a co-owner. (2) You remove her. (3) A co-owner sends her a link. Judged by her demoted row, the co-owner could act on her and bring back a co-owner. `mayActOn` now judges a removed person by her kept level (`keptLevel`). Checking also found the owner's own promotion while she was gone must count. Variant `coowner-brings-back`.

**Left out.** What a client already shows on screen until it refetches (the server refreshes `people` and `leads` on every change), notices already composed before a change, scheduled nudges (they read the person when they run), eligibility and figure masking for company lists, several people at once, devices revoked one by one, groups she was added to by someone else while gone.
