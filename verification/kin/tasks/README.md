# A task's life between people and agents (`node scripts/kin-model.ts tasks`)

**Rules.** An ordinary Work task always has exactly one holder, who can always see it, whatever its alert, privacy or project. The agent never gives a task back to someone who handed it back, unless something on it changed since or the owner said so. A task the owner marks private is seen by the owner, co-owners, its holder and teammates who are also on it. Done can always be reopened. Every hand-off tells whoever receives it exactly once, unless they moved it themselves. Whoever asked the agent for a hand-off gave it, so a hand-back returns to them; the agent is the giver only on its own. "Handed back by" shows only on an open task, naming someone other than its holder, until it is given again or closed.
**Harm guarded.** Work bounces between people and an agent with nothing changing, a hand-off nobody hears of, a private task seen by a teammate, or a task its holder can't see.
**Bound.** One task between the owner, Ana, Ben (on the project) and a lead agent; four hand-offs, one edit or change of who is also on it, its late alert raised and held, private once, moved in and out of Ben's project. About 35,800 states, 3 s. `--variants`: 8/8 caught.
**Fixed.**

1. `NoPingPong`: (1) You give the task to Ana. (2) Ana hands it back: she needs the design. (3) You give it to Ben. (4) Ben hands it back. (5) The agent gives it to Ana again: nothing about the design changed. `notBouncedBack` read only the latest change; it now reads back to the last change that isn't a hand-off. Variant `last-change-only`.
2. `OneNoticePerHandOff`: (1) Ben gives Ana a task on his screen. (2) Ana taps Hand back on hers. It went back to Ben, who heard nothing. A hand-back on the screen now tells the person it goes to: on their Tasks row and in one banner (`assignNotices.tellOnTasks`), never a chat line. Variant `screen-hand-back-silent`.
3. `AskerIsGiver` (owner's decision): (1) Zoya asks the agent to give Arjun a task. (2) Arjun hands it back. It went to the agent, which woke and worked on it, and his screen said the agent gave it. `KinRecords` now sets `assigned_by_agent_id` only when no one asked (`writer.askedBy`), so the asker is the giver ("Zoya gave this to you, via Chief of staff") and gets it back. Variant `agent-gives`.

**Model choices.** Making a task private is one way, as the code has it. Meetings (seen by their attendees) are left out.
**Left out.** Promises and their deal visibility, adding several teammates or an agent to a task, permissions and notices for joining or leaving a task, simultaneous edits to its holder and helpers, several tasks, waiting-on, due days and the pulse's thresholds, delegated tasks, the agent's own turns' views, the holder leaving (in the people model).
Team-list writes apply only to company-list entries. The model covers Work tasks and their hand-offs.
