# A task's life between people and agents (`node scripts/kin-model.ts tasks`)

**Rules.** An ordinary Work task always has exactly one holder, who can always see it, whatever its alert, privacy or project. The agent never gives a task back to someone who handed it back, unless something on it changed since or the owner said so. A task the owner marks private is seen by the owner, co-owners, its holder and teammates who are also on it. Done can always be reopened. Every kept hand-off tells whoever receives it exactly once, unless they moved it themselves. A task one teammate promised another is never held by the person it was promised to: given to them, it is theirs and promised to no one. A task the agent makes is saved at once and tells its holder in that turn, unless the agent holds it itself. A suggestion from before that change stays silent on assignment, even if its holder changes; keeping it tells its current holder. Whoever asked the agent for a hand-off gave it, so a hand-back returns to them; the agent is the giver only on its own. "Handed back by" shows only on an open task, naming someone other than its holder, until it is given again or closed.
**Harm guarded.** Work bounces between people and an agent with nothing changing, a hand-off nobody hears of, a private task seen by a teammate, or a task its holder can't see.
**Bound.** One task between the owner, Ana, Ben (on the project) and a lead agent; four hand-offs, one edit or change of who is also on it, its late alert raised and held, private once, moved in and out of Ben's project. The task starts as the owner's own, as one the agent made for anyone (saved, its holder told, the agent the giver unless someone asked), or as a suggestion from before that change, kept once; it may start promised to Ana or Ben, never its holder. About 245,000 states, 9 s. `--variants`: 12/12 caught.
**Fixed.**

1. `NoPingPong`: (1) You give the task to Ana. (2) Ana hands it back: she needs the design. (3) You give it to Ben. (4) Ben hands it back. (5) The agent gives it to Ana again: nothing about the design changed. `notBouncedBack` read only the latest change; it now reads back to the last change that isn't a hand-off. Variant `last-change-only`.
2. `OneNoticePerHandOff`: (1) Ben gives Ana a task on his screen. (2) Ana taps Hand back on hers. It went back to Ben, who heard nothing. A hand-back on the screen now tells the person it goes to: on their Tasks row and in one banner (`assignNotices.tellOnTasks`), never a chat line. Variant `screen-hand-back-silent`.
3. `AskerIsGiver` (owner's decision): (1) Zoya asks the agent to give Arjun a task. (2) Arjun hands it back. It went to the agent, which woke and worked on it, and his screen said the agent gave it. `KinRecords` now sets `assigned_by_agent_id` only when no one asked (`writer.askedBy`), so the asker is the giver ("Zoya gave this to you, via Chief of staff") and gets it back. Variant `agent-gives`.

4. `NoSuggestedNotice`: giving an unkept suggestion to an agent could wake them before anyone kept it. Variant `suggested-wakes`.
5. `NoPingPong` (fixed 2026-11-25, `221707c5c8`): (1) Ana holds a task she promised Ben. (2) She hands it back: she needs the design. (3) The agent gives it to Ben, the person it was promised to. (4) The agent gives it straight back to Ana: nothing about the design changed. Giving it to Ben cleared its `promisedTo` (`promisedOff`, #839) in the hand-off's own logged change, and `notBouncedBack` read that line as a change on the task; coming off `alsoOn` because it was given to that teammate (`alsoOnFor`) did the same. In a change with an `owner` line, `isHandOffByProduct` (`recordRows.ts`) now counts a `promisedTo` line clearing a promise to the new holder, an `alsoOn` line that only loses the new holder, and an `untold` line that clears as the give, not a change. Tests "G13: given in between to the person it was promised to, a task still doesn't go straight back", "G13: given in between to someone already on it, a task still doesn't go straight back" and "G13: a new promise made after a hand-back is a change, so the task may go back". Variant `byproduct-is-change`. Compared: `recordRows.ts:805-841` (`HAND_OFF_FIELDS`, `isHandOffByProduct`, the stop in `notBouncedBack`) with `Tasks.tla` `Give` (lines 70-80: `bounced` kept across a give, `helper` and `promised` cleared for the receiver) and `HandBack` (lines 103-105), `promised` (line 31) and `NoPingPong` (line 197); the receiver is the owner line's `to` in the code and `to` in the model. The model folds the `alsoOn` and `promisedTo` by-products into one condition (`to \in {helper, promised}`) and has no `untold`: each is a field cleared because the task went to its receiver, which `NoPingPong` cannot tell apart, so a separate step would only grow the state space.

**Model choices.** Making a task private is one way, as the code has it. Who a task is promised to is set when it is made; setting or changing it later is an edit like any other. Meetings (seen by their attendees) are left out.
**Left out.** A client's promises and their deal visibility, the late notice a promised person gets (the checkin model's held post), adding several teammates or an agent to a task, permissions and notices for joining or leaving a task, simultaneous edits to its holder and helpers, several tasks, waiting-on, due days and the pulse's thresholds, delegated tasks, the agent's own turns' views, the holder leaving (in the people model).
Team-list writes apply only to company-list entries. The model covers Work tasks and their hand-offs.
