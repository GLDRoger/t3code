# Send card (`node scripts/kin-model.ts send`)

**Rules.** A mail confirmed from a company mailbox goes out at most once, exactly as its card showed it, from the account its card showed, never in an outdated version, and only on the owner's or a co-owner's Confirm. An agent's correction replaces the older card for good. Confirm always ends as Sent or Failed. A failed or outdated mail comes back on a new card, or on the agent's newer card for it. If the previous try may have gone, Confirm requires acknowledging the sent-mail check for that try.
**Harm guarded.** Money or mail leaves the company twice, outdated, unasked, or not at all, and nobody can tell.
**Bound.** Two agent cards (a draft and a correction), five card slots, and at most two of these, once each: the invoice is re-made, the sign-in expires, the account is signed in again as someone else Kin can't name, the mail service fails, Kin restarts. About 11,300 states, 12 s. `--variants` breaks each rule on purpose (13/13 caught).
**Fixed.** Kin restarting between "the card says it failed" and the new card left the mail on no card. The failure now sets `kin_connection_asks.owes_card` in its own transaction; binding the new card (or finding the agent's newer card) clears it, and start-up posts every owed card (`interruptedSends`). The new card needs a fresh Confirm. Variant `no-owed-card`.
**Fixed.** A sign-in again whose account Google couldn't name kept the new tokens under the old account's name, so a card made for A, open or already sending, went out from B. Reconnecting a mail account now refuses an unreadable identity before any token is kept, and each ask stores the account and `sign_in_gen` its card showed; Confirm and the send (after the token is read) check both, and a mismatch fails safe with the same mail on a new card naming the account. In the model a refused sign-in changes nothing, so `SignInAsOther` only moves in its variants: `old-reconnect` (the code before) and `no-account-bind`.
**"Comes back".** This includes the mail standing on the agent's own newer card: a correction is the same mail.
**Left out.** Watched-number cards, personal mailboxes and disconnection, edits or address changes on a card, section switches, calendar and MCP-call cards (they never come back on a card). The From switch. Receipts and statements: their Confirm records inside the tap's own transaction, so a restart can't split it. A co-owner's tap runs the same `ownerLevel` check as the owner's, so the model has one owner-level person.
