# A turn's reply (`node scripts/kin-model.ts reply`)

**Rules.** The reply is the text the agent writes, wherever it falls among its tool calls; the turn ends when the agent stops. Each message gets at most one reply or one plain failure line, and eventually at least one, or a chart or file whose agent was asked for its line, even if the agent sent the asker a nudge or relay meanwhile. After a reply, a later failure adds at most one short line, and only to name what the reply promised and the turn left undone (`NoteOnlyForBrokenPromise`, the owner's decision). Reasoning and terminal provider diagnostics never post as the reply. An answer written again after a tool call is said once (`SaidOnce`). A turn that ends without the words a message waited for, having only thought them or only posted a chart or file, asks the agent for them once (`AskedBeforeSilence`). A nudge or relay always lands whatever the floor. "Working…" always clears. When someone joins while the agent works and its reply is held back, the message goes to a fresh turn at most once (`MovedOnce`), nothing written for the old audience posts (`NeverToOldAudience`), and the message still ends in one answer or one line. A join that expires asks still waiting, or held by a turn it stopped, tells the chat once.
**Harm guarded.** A person waits for an answer that never comes, gets two, or reads the agent's private reasoning.
**Bound.** Two messages from one person to one lead, four start attempts, one restart, a floor that can close, a reply that may promise a card or hand-over, and up to two joins (the second needs a leave between, so a fresh turn's prompt is owner-only again), each by the lead's owner (the turn is spared, `SomeoneJoins`) or by someone else (the turn is stopped, `JoinStops`). About 711,000 states, 65 s. `--variants`: 15/15 caught.
**Answered.** When a reply posts, the bridge sets `kin_events.replied_at` on the messages the model had read before it (`markReplied`: the claim's own, then each steer once its message shows in the thread). The model's `Steer` is the claim and that read in one step.
