# A turn's reply (`node scripts/kin-model.ts reply`)

**Rules.** Each message gets at most one reply or one plain failure line, and eventually at least one, even if the agent sent the asker a nudge or relay meanwhile. After a reply, a later failure adds at most one short line, and only to name what the reply promised and the turn left undone (`NoteOnlyForBrokenPromise`, the owner's decision). Reasoning never posts as the reply. A nudge or relay always lands whatever the floor. "Working…" always clears.
**Harm guarded.** A person waits for an answer that never comes, gets two, or reads the agent's private reasoning.
**Bound.** Two messages from one person to one lead, three start attempts, one restart, a floor that can close, a reply that may promise a card or hand-over. About 5,600 states, 4 s. `--variants`: 6/6 caught.
**Answered.** When a reply posts, the bridge sets `kin_events.replied_at` on the messages the model had read before it (`markReplied`: the claim's own, then each steer once its message shows in the thread). The model's `Steer` is the claim and that read in one step.
**Fixed.**

1. The reply posted, a later step failed, and "{Lead} stopped partway." followed as a second answer. A replied message is now done (`KinEventQueue.finish` `replied`); the runner's `replied` outcome posts a line only for a card the reply named and never posted (`missingCard`) or a hand-over that never happened (`unkeptPromise`), else it logs. Variant `partway-after-reply`.
2. Kin shut down after the reply, the message was requeued, and the agent answered again. `requeueUnfinished` (and `release`) now mark replied messages done. Variant `requeue-replied`.
3. A nudge or relay sent to the asker mid-turn counted as their reply (`KinReplyBridge.answered`), leaving them with silence. Variant `relay-counts`.
4. The reply posted and its `replied_at` were two writes, so a crash between them requeued an answered message. The mark now runs in the post's own transaction (the gate's `bind`), which is the model's one `Post` step. Test `a reply and its answered mark commit together`.
   **Left out.** Several leads in a group, steering caps, rescue timing, reply-text classification (`classify.ts`), non-Claude reasoning ids, hangs before a run starts, and the lane-wide "stopped partway" line when one of two messages was replied.
