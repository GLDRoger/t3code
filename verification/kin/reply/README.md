# A turn's reply (`node scripts/kin-model.ts reply`)

**Rules.** The reply is the text the agent writes, wherever it falls among its tool calls; the turn ends when the agent stops. Kin holds that text while the run works and posts it only once the run has ended (`HeldToTheEnd`), as one group of 1 to 4 bubbles, or parts of a long page, in one transaction: the group shows whole or not at all, whatever restarts (`AllOrNone`), and rings once, with bubble 1 (`OneRing`). A send (`kin_send`) is the agent's own message and posts when it is sent; it is the turn's one message, so text written beside it posts nothing. A turn a trigger started (no person's event among those it claimed, with `kinTriggerSendOnly` on, its default) speaks only through the send: its plain text posts nothing (`TriggerOnlySends`), and a completed run that wrote text and neither sent nor skipped is asked once to send it or end with `kin_skip` (`NudgeOnce`). After a person's Stop nothing that run held for their message posts (`StopPostsNothing`); a failed run still posts the finished text it held and is not asked again. Each message gets at most one reply, however many bubbles, or one plain failure line, and eventually at least one, or a chart or file whose agent was asked for its line, even if the agent sent the asker a nudge or relay meanwhile. After a reply, a later failure adds at most one short line, and only to name what the reply promised and the turn left undone (`NoteOnlyForBrokenPromise`, the owner's decision). An agent may end its turn with `kin_skip` and post nothing only where no answer is owed (a plain reaction, another agent's answer coming back, a message it handed off); elsewhere the skip is refused and the reply posts as usual. Reasoning and terminal provider diagnostics never post as the reply. An answer written again after a tool call is said once (`SaidOnce`). A turn that ends without the words a message waited for, having only thought them or only posted a chart or file, asks the agent for them once (`AskedBeforeSilence`). A nudge or relay always lands whatever the floor. "Working…" always clears. When someone joins while the agent works and its reply is held back, the message goes to a fresh turn at most once (`MovedOnce`), nothing written for the old audience posts (`NeverToOldAudience`), and the message still ends in one answer or one line. A join that expires asks still waiting, or held by a turn it stopped, tells the chat once.
**Harm guarded.** A person waits for an answer that never comes, gets two, reads the agent's private reasoning, reads half a reply, is rung once per bubble, or presses Stop and still gets the answer they stopped.
**Bound.** Two messages from one person to one lead, four start attempts, one restart (the run gone, so its messages are requeued, or the run still in the thread after it ended, so the wake-up is picked up again), a Stop, a send and a `kin_skip` per wake-up, message 2 the kind that may go unanswered (`Quiet`) or a trigger's event instead (`Triggers`, chosen as it arrives, so a person's message may steer into a trigger's wake-up), one nudge (two in `nudge-loops`), a floor that can close (only where a relay through it would read it, `relay-through-floor`), a reply that may promise a card or hand-over, and up to two joins (the second needs a leave between, so a fresh turn's prompt is owner-only again), each by the lead's owner (the turn is spared, `SomeoneJoins`) or by someone else (the turn is stopped, `JoinStops`), and the asker made a member from co-owner once (`MadeMember`). About 1,555,000 states, 508 s (2026-11-27, with `Send`, once a finished turn's leftovers are cleared). `--variants`: 28/28 caught, 44 s.
**Answered.** When a reply posts, the bridge sets `kin_events.replied_at` on the messages the model had read before it (`markReplied`: the claim's own, then each steer once its message shows in the thread). The model's `Steer` is the claim and that read in one step.
