# A discussion in the Team room (`node scripts/kin-model.ts discussion`)

**Rules.** Every discussion ends, decided, parked or brought to the owner, and after its last allowed message only the closing one is left. Agents have at most 2 discussions open at once and start at most 6 a day; the owner's own ask always opens. One message per turn. At most one agent nobody asked speaks after any one message. A decision can be objected to once. A teammate never reads the Team room, whoever its membership rows list. The owner is told each outcome exactly once: at once when he asked for it or when it is brought to him, else beside his next check-in. A hand-off's answer always comes back to the agent who asked, once.
**Harm guarded.** Agents talking without end or spending past their share, a pile-up of discussions, everyone chiming in on everything, an objection used to reopen a decision forever, exact figures read by a teammate, the owner told twice or never, a hand-off answered where the asker never sees it.
**Bound.** One discussion between four agents (opener, decider, chair, a stakeholder who may pitch in), its message cap scaled to 5 with the chair check at 3 and two messages after an objection; before it, agent-started discussions against the real caps (2 at once, 6 a day), a co-owner and a member promoted and demoted three times, and one hand-off whose receiver may fail and be retried. About 11,000 states, 70 s. `--variants`: 11/11 caught.
**Fixed.**

1. `OneObjection`: (1) Social opens it. (2) Finance answers. (3) The Chief of staff decides. (4) Finance objects and decides in the same message. (5) Social is offered an objection too. An objection may no longer carry an end. Variant `object-decides`.
2. `EndsWithinCaps`: (1) Five messages in, Finance decides at the limit. (2) Social objects. (3) The closing message is the seventh. A decision at the limit now stands without an objection. Variant `objection-past-cap`.

**Model choices.** The owner may add anyone to the room here, though `addMember` refuses it: the viewer rule alone must keep a teammate out. Who has a stake is chosen freely per message, as the words of a message are. The decider answers whoever spoke before it. Units spent and a quiet day are one action (`Cut`): each leaves only the closing turn. Owner-level access is the people model's; here it only flips.
**Left out.** Several discussions at once (counted, not followed), the daily budget share (a refusal like the caps), what agents say, the disagreement card's buttons, pushes and quiet hours (the told-once and quiet-hours models' own).
