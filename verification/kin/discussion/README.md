# A discussion in the Team room (`node scripts/kin-model.ts discussion`)

**Rules.** Every discussion ends, decided, parked or brought to the owner, and after its last allowed message only the closing one is left. Agents have at most 2 discussions open at once and start at most 6 a day; the owner's own ask always opens. The owner's Team-room message naming two agents opens their discussion with no opening turn, the first-named speaking first; it gets one answer, that discussion or (while another is open) an ordinary reply, never both and never twice, however often it is handled, and the agent whose turn it is there cannot open a second for it. One message per turn. At most one agent nobody asked speaks after any one message. A decision can be objected to once. A teammate never reads the Team room, whoever its membership rows list. The owner is told each outcome exactly once: at once when he asked for it or when it is brought to him, else beside his next check-in. A hand-off's answer always comes back to the agent that passed it, once, when it asked for the answer; one it didn't ask for is not copied back (`UnaskedStaysThere`, the owner's decision: what one agent passes another is that agent's input, not a line in a person's chat).
**Harm guarded.** Agents talking without end or spending past their share, a pile-up of discussions, everyone chiming in on everything, an objection used to reopen a decision forever, exact figures read by a teammate, the owner told twice or never, a hand-off answered where the asker never sees it, the owner's ask answered alone beside the discussion it opened, or opening two.
**Bound.** One discussion between four agents (opener, decider, chair, a stakeholder who may pitch in), opened by an agent for the owner, on its own, or by the owner's message naming the opener and the decider or the chair; its message cap scaled to 5 and two messages after an objection; before it, agent-started discussions against the real caps (2 at once, 6 a day), the owner's message handled twice, a co-owner and a member promoted and demoted three times, and one hand-off, its answer asked for or not, whose receiver may fail and be retried. 44,410 states, 32 s (2026-11-27, with the decider's bring and no chair check, once a message is tried only by the agent whose turn it is, with only the values it reads). `--variants`: 16/16 caught, 23 s.
**Fixed.**

1. `OneObjection`: (1) Social opens it. (2) Finance answers. (3) The Chief of staff decides. (4) Finance objects and decides in the same message. (5) Social is offered an objection too. An objection may no longer carry an end. Variant `object-decides`.
2. `EndsWithinCaps`: (1) Five messages in, Finance decides at the limit. (2) Social objects. (3) The closing message is the seventh. A decision at the limit now stands without an objection. Variant `objection-past-cap`.
3. `AskAnsweredOnce`: (1) An agent opens a discussion. (2) The owner's message naming two agents is answered as an ordinary message. (3) That discussion ends. (4) The message is handled again after a restart and opens a discussion beside the reply. A message once answered plainly now stays so. Variant `plain-forgotten`.
4. `AskAnsweredOnce`: (1) The owner's message names Social and Finance; Kin opens their discussion, Social's turn. (2) Social, told that such an ask starts with `team_open`, opens a second for the same ask. `KinTeam.open` now refuses an agent that holds the turn in an open discussion a person's message opened, naming it and `team_say`. Variant `open-beside`.

**Model choices.** The owner may add anyone to the room here, though `addMember` refuses it: the viewer rule alone must keep a teammate out. Who has a stake is chosen freely per message, as the words of a message are. The decider answers whoever spoke before it. Whose call it is stays D whoever the owner's message names (the code makes it the Chief of staff when named, else the first-named), and a plain reply is one flag, as ChatWake's event keyed by message and agent is. Units spent and a quiet day are one action (`Cut`): each leaves only the closing turn. Owner-level access is the people model's; here it only flips.
**Left out.** Several discussions at once (counted, not followed), the daily budget share (a refusal like the caps), what agents say, the disagreement card's buttons, scheduled run reports, number-crossing wakes and feeds, the waiting list’s representation of text and card questions, pushes and quiet hours (the told-once and quiet-hours models' own).
