# Loose check-in, a fact told once, and quiet hours (`node scripts/kin-model.ts checkin`)

**Rules.** A day's check-in comes at most once, never before its window, at a minute inside the window (or at once, when Kin only noticed the slot after its window closed), and it happens if Kin runs at any time in the window. A moved check-in stays moved. A fact the owner hasn't been told as it stands now, even one in hand, is offered to that day's check-in. An unchanged fact is told at most once. A completed check-in that omits the fact releases its reservation so the next check-in can offer it again. Holding a fact never hides the task. A post nobody asked for at a time it must wait, a relay, nudge or notice included, is held, stored, and delivered exactly once, when its reader is free: never in their quiet hours, and for someone holding messages outside their working hours (a member, by default), never outside those, each on their own clock. A line someone left for the owner's next check-in is a held post the check-in takes: told there once and closed, freed again when that check-in posted nothing, and posted as a message only when no check-in took it by a day later.
**Harm guarded.** The owner's morning check-in repeats, comes early, silently doesn't come, leaves out something new, or says the same thing again or keeps a fact reserved after leaving it out; a night line wakes him or a teammate, a line lands on a teammate's evening or day off, is lost on a restart, or arrives twice; a line left for his check-in is told there and sent as a message too, or is lost when the check-in says nothing.
**Bound.** Two days in five-minute ticks (the first four of each day are quiet hours), one outage, one 15-minute clock change (never in the seconds between a slot falling due and the scheduler running it), one move, one fact, one unprompted post, to the owner or to a member whose clock runs half a day ahead and who works ticks 6 to 9 of her day, or instead one line left for the owner's check-in on the first day, before its window or after it. About 94 million states, 21 min. The chart is drawn on one day without clock changes. `--variants`: 17/17 caught.
**Fixed.**

1. `FiresWhenUp`: the host slept from before the window until 9:10, T3's scheduler skipped the run as more than 10 minutes late, and the day's check-in never came. T3's scheduler is unchanged; on Kin's scheduled-tasks tick (start-up included), `checkInCatchUp.ts missedCheckIns` puts the fire the slot would have made (`ns:<task>:<window start>:scheduled`, so once) when T3 has moved past today's slot, it has not run since its window opened, the window opened under 3 hours ago that day, and the owner is in their hours. Variant `no-catch-up`.
2. `InsideWindow`: a late start picked its minute from the start, up to 10 minutes past the window. `queueCheckIn` now picks from the window itself (`looseWindowStartAt` + offset) and fires at once when that minute has passed. The rule now allows "at once" for a slot Kin only noticed after its window closed, which the catch-up makes possible (owner's decision). Variant `late-offset`; `fire-early` now also picks from the early start, since the window pick alone keeps an early run from waking early.
3. `FactNeverAbsent`: a check-in whose only open fact was held said "nothing new". `queueCheckIn` now counts held facts (`includeHandled`); one marked not a problem starts none while it lasts (for good, or for an invoice still owed until its next chase). Variant `held-left-out`.
4. `QuietIsQuiet`: a line held through the night, with the host asleep all day, posted the moment it woke in the next night's quiet hours. `heldDelivery.ts deliverHeld` now holds it again until that night ends. Variant `deliver-late-night`.
