# Loose check-in, a fact told once, and quiet hours (`node scripts/kin-model.ts checkin`)

**Rules.** A day's check-in comes at most once, never before its window, at a minute inside the window (or at once, when Kin only noticed the slot after its window closed), and it happens if Kin runs at any time in the window. A moved check-in stays moved. A fact the owner hasn't been told as it stands now, even one in hand, is in that day's check-in; an unchanged fact is told at most once. Holding a fact never hides the task. A post nobody asked for in the owner's quiet hours is held, stored, and delivered exactly once, never inside quiet hours.
**Harm guarded.** The owner's morning check-in repeats, comes early, silently doesn't come, leaves out something new, or says the same thing again; a night line wakes him, is lost on a restart, or arrives twice.
**Bound.** Two days in five-minute ticks (the first four of each day are quiet hours), one outage, one 15-minute clock change (never in the seconds between a slot falling due and the scheduler running it), one move, one fact, one unprompted post. About 12 million states, 95 s. The chart is drawn on one day without clock changes. `--variants`: 12/12 caught.
**Fixed.**

1. `FiresWhenUp`: the host slept from before the window until 9:10, T3's scheduler skipped the run as more than 10 minutes late, and the day's check-in never came. T3's scheduler is unchanged; on Kin's scheduled-tasks tick (start-up included), `checkInCatchUp.ts missedCheckIns` puts the fire the slot would have made (`ns:<task>:<window start>:scheduled`, so once) when T3 has moved past today's slot, it has not run since its window opened, the window opened under 3 hours ago that day, and the owner is in their hours. Variant `no-catch-up`.
2. `InsideWindow`: a late start picked its minute from the start, up to 10 minutes past the window. `queueCheckIn` now picks from the window itself (`looseWindowStartAt` + offset) and fires at once when that minute has passed. The rule now allows "at once" for a slot Kin only noticed after its window closed, which the catch-up makes possible (owner's decision). Variant `late-offset`; `fire-early` now also picks from the early start, since the window pick alone keeps an early run from waking early.
3. `FactNeverAbsent`: a check-in whose only open fact was held said "nothing new". `queueCheckIn` now counts held facts (`includeHandled`); one marked not a problem has no end and starts none. Variant `held-left-out`.
4. `QuietIsQuiet`: a line held through the night, with the host asleep all day, posted the moment it woke in the next night's quiet hours. `heldDelivery.ts deliverHeld` now holds it again until that night ends. Variant `deliver-late-night`.
   **Told once.** `FactNeverAbsent` was "every open fact is in each check-in", which made agents repeat themselves; the owner changed the promise to "told once, again only when it changed" (`told.ts`, `kin_pulse_told`). `ToldOnce` guards it (variant `tell-again`); `HeldDeliveredOnce`, `HeldNeverLost` and `QuietIsQuiet` guard the hold (`deliver-twice`, `held-in-memory`, `post-in-quiet`).
   **Model change.** A move re-measures today against the new window (`Remeasured`): being up in the old window no longer counts once it moved.
   **Left out.** Outside mail lines (their cursor moves even when a same-day duplicate drops them), the 200-fact cap, member-vs-owner hold visibility, not-a-problem marks, Run now, a failed turn untelling its facts, a threshold crossed again or a due day (each just another "changed"), merging several held lines, the owner's hours on the catch-up, DST.
