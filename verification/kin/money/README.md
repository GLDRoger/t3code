# Money recorded on a Confirm (`node scripts/kin-model.ts money`)

**Rules.** Only the owner's or a co-owner's Confirm records money, at most once per receipt card however many phones tap it, in the amount the card showed, and never from a card whose invoice changed since it was drawn. An invoice's received total never goes above its amount or above the money that really came in. A statement row is recorded once, however many times its file is read, sent again or confirmed. A confirmed receipt is recorded even if Kin restarts right after the tap.
**Harm guarded.** Money is counted twice, counted from a stale or unconfirmed card, or confirmed and then lost.
**Bound.** One client transfer of ₹1 lakh against an invoice of ₹2 lakh, which an agent's receipt card (at most two cards) and a bank statement row both stand for; the statement may be read three times in all (again, or the same file sent again), each read asks its currency; at most two of these, once each: the invoice is edited, a payment taken back, the statement undone, Kin restarts. About 935,000 states, 22 s. The chart is drawn with two reads and one of those. `--variants`: 9/9 caught.
**Fixed.**

1. `MoneyInBounded`: (1) Kin reads the bank statement and you pick its currency; the card matches the row to the invoice. (2) The agent puts up a receipt card for the same money and you confirm it: the invoice has received ₹1 lakh. (3) You confirm the statement card, still open from before: the invoice has received ₹2 lakh for one payment. The statement card matched against what the invoice owed at the read. Each invoice match now keeps the invoice's amount and the payments others recorded on it (`Candidate.seen`, `invoiceSeen`); Confirm refuses ("Something changed since this card was made") when they moved. The model first caught a balance-only check out too: undo the other statement's payment, then confirm the receipt, and the invoice owes what it owed at the read again. Variant `no-seen-check`.

**Model choices.** The matcher is the model's: a row already recorded on the invoice by a receipt is offered as recorded, otherwise the invoice is paid while it owes. The agent asks for a receipt only for money not yet recorded (`proposalOutcome` and the invoice tell it).
**Left out.** Advances with no invoice: two advance cards for one payment no longer both record, since each card's snapshot holds the same sum from the same client on the same day already on the books and shows it; that is tested in `gendocs/DocJobs.test.ts` ("record money an agent asks for only on the owner's Confirm"), not modelled. Also left out: section switches, shared receipts appearing or disappearing from Work views, watched-number cards, partner reimbursements, bank alert reconciliation, several invoices or rows, invoice number matching, TDS, foreign amounts, partial matches the owner picks, expenses, receipts on a payment that need no card, the receipt's PDF (made after the tap; asking again makes it), statement passwords. How an invoice comes to be owed: the model's invoice is issued before it starts, as a statement offers only an issued (`waiting`) invoice; an agent's invoice is a draft until the owner or a co-owner issues it.
Team-list visibility leaves invoice and receipt authority in this model unchanged. The shared list rule excludes both lists.
