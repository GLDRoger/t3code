# A question an agent asks one person (`node scripts/kin-model.ts questions`)

**Rules.** While a question is open, no agent asks the same thing again. A default is never taken before its day, and at most once however often Kin restarts. Once answered, the default is never taken. Nothing that leaves the company or moves money is done by a default. An open question's card takes taps until the question closes. A question with no safe default is raised once more, never twice. Every question ends: answered, its default taken and done, withdrawn, or raised once and resting in Waiting on you.
**Harm guarded.** Finance and the Chief of staff both asking whether to draft the same reminder; a default taken twice after a restart, or after the person already tapped; a reminder sent to a client because nobody answered; a no-default question raised every morning forever; a card that stops taking taps a day before the question's own day.
**Bound.** Finance and the Chief of staff, two question slots about the same record and intent, days of two ticks, a seven-tick horizon, one restart. About 92,000 states, 15 s. `--variants`: 8/8 caught.
**Model choices.** Time is ticks: a day is two, the raise one tick after the day. The sweep (`settleDue`) is one atomic step per question, as its conditional update in one transaction is. "Leaves the company" is a truth the agent may hide; `safeDefault`'s word check is the guard the model trusts, so its misses are a test question, not a model one.
**Left out.** The word check's false negatives (tested in `rules.test.ts`); the agent's Confirm card for anything that does leave the company; text questions, which lapse silently at their day and are never taken; working days and zones (tested in `rules.test.ts`); group chats, where no question is stored.
