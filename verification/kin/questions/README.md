# A question an agent asks one person (`node scripts/kin-model.ts questions`)

**Rules.** While a question is open, no agent asks the same thing again. For seven days after a dismissal, a repeat through `kin_ask` is refused unless the person brought the subject up again. A default is never taken before its day, and at most once however often Kin restarts. Once answered, the default is never taken. Nothing that leaves the company or moves money is done by a default. An open question's card takes taps until the question closes. A question with no safe default is raised once more, never twice. Every question ends: answered, its default taken and done, withdrawn, dismissed, lapsed, or (asked for the agent itself) raised once and resting in Waiting on you. Asked for another chat (the asker lane), a question has no default, a held one is posted before it is raised, and its answer, by tap or in words, wakes the asker once in the chat that asked and never starts a turn in the answerer's own chat, however Kin restarts. Asked for another chat and unanswered, it lapses at its raise's own day, never before, or at its first day instead of a raise when its person never signed in, who is never asked again; the chat it was asked for is told once, with the asker's one turn there. The exceptions, both on a teammate's sent-up draft, are outside the model (see Left out).
**Harm guarded.** Finance and the Chief of staff both asking whether to draft the same reminder; a default taken twice after a restart, or after the person already tapped; a reminder sent to a client because nobody answered; a no-default question raised every morning forever; a card that stops taking taps a day before the question's own day; Kin answering Meera's question for her; Meera's tap waking the agent in her chat as well as Rhea's; an answer lost between the tap and the turn that carries it on; Dev's question to Zoya dying quietly after its raise, or told to Dev's chat twice; Arjun, who never signed in, asked a second time in a chat he never opens.
**Bound.** Finance and the Chief of staff, two question slots about the same record and intent (Finance asks in the first, the Chief of staff in the second), days of two ticks, a seven-tick horizon, a dismissal window scaled to three ticks, one restart. Either question may be asked for another chat, held first, and of someone who never signed in; one asked for another chat leaves a day after its raise, and time waits for that raise. 21,945 states, about 9 s. `--variants`: 16 variants, the four lapse ones each caught on their own, about 23 s.
**Model choices.** Time is ticks: a day is two, the raise one tick after the day. The sweep (`settleDue`) is one atomic step per question, as its conditional update in one transaction is. "Leaves the company" is a truth the agent may hide; `safeDefault`'s word check is the guard the model trusts, so its misses are a test question, not a model one.
**Left out.** The word check's false negatives (tested in `rules.test.ts`); the agent's Confirm card for anything that does leave the company; text questions, which lapse at their day (stored as lapsed, nobody told) and are never taken, and how their waiting rows merge into a card; watched-number cards; working days, hours and zones (a person's own hours now end their day and start a raise, tested in `rules.test.ts`); Undo dismissal, the visual closure of a settled card on tap, how the server matches a dismissed question or recognises that the person brought the subject up again, task follow-ups after a Yes, group chats, where no question is stored; who may ask whom for another chat (tested in `openQuestions.test.ts`); the asker's quiet hours, where the answer's line and turn land at once and only their push is silent (`KinPushSender`). A teammate's sent-up draft (`draft_json`) never lapses: it waits on the owner after its raise as before (`raise`). The owner's Approve, by the stored kind of Kin's own Approve button, at most once per question (`approveDraft`, `approved_at`), writes a proposal or client terms as a doc itself, word for word, in the tap's own transaction, with no turn, and the tap fails whole if that write does (`sentUpDraft.ts` `writeApproved`); any other kind queues one turn in his own chat, at his level, claimed alone in its own lane, instead of one in the chat that asked (read back only through `approval.ts` `approvalOf`); his Changes first, tapped or taken from his own words, queues no turn at all, only Kin's line in the chat that asked (`changesFirst`), and his redraft from his own chat, in a turn on his own words there, is a new question asked for that chat (`askPerson` `redo`, `redoOf`); any other answer, in words included, is as modelled. That is tested in `openQuestions.test.ts`.
