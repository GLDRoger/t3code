# Work's live stream to one person (`node scripts/kin-model.ts stream`)

**Rules.** Once everything sent has arrived, what a person's open Tasks holds equals a fresh read of their own view, whatever order the changes arrived in and however often the socket dropped. A member is never told of a task they can't see: a removal goes only to someone told of it before. A change of level starts the screen over before anything else reaches it. If the section switches while the socket is down, the promise's client label also equals a fresh read after resume. The resume number stays below a section switch until the host tells the client to read the view again.
**Harm guarded.** A task that changed while a phone was reconnecting and never shows the change; a removal that tells a member a hidden task exists; a demoted co-owner who keeps getting tasks she no longer sees.
**Bound.** Ana, two tasks including one promise linked to a deal, three row edits, shares, deal label changes or section switches, one change of level, one dropped socket; ticks publish in any order. About 646,000 states, 9 s. `--variants`: 8/8 caught.
**Fixed.**

1. `StreamEqualsRead`: (1) Task a changes, then task b. (2) b's tick publishes first: afterCommit callbacks run once the write lock is free, so a later commit can publish first. (3) The host sends b and tells her its number. (4) Her socket drops; a's tick publishes to no one. (5) She resumes from b's number: nothing new, so a never comes. Each burst now closes at the last number every earlier change has reached the session by (`resumable` in `workStream.ts`). Variant `close-at-top`.
2. `StreamEqualsRead`: (1) Task a changes; her socket drops and she starts over at that number. (2) a is taken from her view. (3) a's first tick arrives late: the session reads a as hidden and sends its removal at the late tick's number, which her client drops as older than what it holds, while the session forgets she holds a. (4) The second tick sends nothing. a stays on her screen. Rows now carry the number read just before them (`rowsNow`). Variant `tick-number`.

3. `StreamEqualsRead`: Ana has a promise in Tasks. Its deal becomes private and only the deal tick arrives, leaving the promise on her screen. `KinRecords.update` now includes linked promises in the write’s visibility scope. The deal tick names those promise rows so `pendingOf` and `rowsNow` send ordinary removals or upserts. A public deal sends the current promise even if it was edited while hidden and all the ticks reached Ana after it became public. Variant `deal-no-dependent-row`.

4. `StreamEqualsRead`: switching Deals off hides linked promises on a fresh read. `KinShell.set` now calls the Work stream’s whole-view announcement when an effective section switch changes. The burst starts the screen over, including when Deals comes back on. Variant `no-shell-refresh`.

5. `ClientLabelEqualsRead`: a co-owner disconnects, then Clients turns off. The switch reserves a sequence without writing a record change. `start` now checks replay through SQLite's allocated sequence, so the missing number triggers a fresh read. Variant `skip-reserved-on-resume`.

6. `SectionSwitchBeforeCursor`. A task tick reaches a co-owner ahead of the Clients switch announcement. The row can arrive, but `resumable` holds the cursor below the unaccounted sequence so a reconnect still finds the hole. If the next burst encounters the same gap, the host requests a fresh read. Variant `skip-live-reserved`.

**Model choices.** Her numbering is one counter in both levels; the code switches between her own and the org's, which `afterTeam` keeps apart and a start-over covers. A co-owner's replay is bounded by `REPLAY_LIMIT` in the code and not here. A people tick takes a number of its own here, where the code reuses the latest change's.
**Left out.** Counts (deltas against what the client holds, proven by the property test), several people at once, list pages and their windows (the property test folds the real reducers against real reads), client and project decorations other than the linked promise's label after a switch while offline, receipts following the client or project of their payment source, settings-dependent content outside Tasks, `announce` naming the clients or deals a contact log or a deal edit moved (the original record change carries the promise keys), and an older phone's `changed` stream, the same session's sends told as names to read again (`subscribeChanged`).
Team-list definitions refresh through the workLists topic. The model covers the numbered Work stream.
